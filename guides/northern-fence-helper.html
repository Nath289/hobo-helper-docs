Northern Fence Helper

Assists in navigating the Northern Fence by replacing broken banners, tracking Super-Cart Racing skill gains over time, and adding quick-race and bank calculation buttons for Pikies.

What does it do?

Assists in navigating the Northern Fence by replacing broken banners, tracking Super-Cart Racing skill gains over time, and adding quick-race and bank calculation buttons for Pikies.

How to disable it

If you prefer to disable this functionality, you can easily turn it off:

  • Go to the Preferences page in-game.
  • Scroll down to the Hobo Helper Settings section.
  • Uncheck the relevant box and hit Save.
