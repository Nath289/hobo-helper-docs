Display Helper

A global display improvement suite that widens the content area, rounds avatars with online pulses, extracts custom navigation banners, and makes topbars swipeable on mobile.

What does it do?

How to disable it

If you prefer to disable this functionality, you can easily turn it off:

  • Go to the Preferences page in-game.
  • Scroll down to the Hobo Helper Settings section.
  • Uncheck the relevant box and hit Save.
