Mines Helper

A massive suite of tools for the Canbodian Mines. Widens the view, highlights safe zones, styles navigation links, and adds an offline persistence log for ores found and EXP gained.

What does it do?

A massive suite of tools for the Canbodian Mines. Widens the view, highlights safe zones, styles navigation links, and adds an offline persistence log for ores found and EXP gained.

How to disable it

If you prefer to disable this functionality, you can easily turn it off:

  • Go to the Preferences page in-game.
  • Scroll down to the Hobo Helper Settings section.
  • Uncheck the relevant box and hit Save.
