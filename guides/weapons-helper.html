Weapons Helper

Highlights items in your inventory that are currently equipped and adds instant quick equip/unequip clickable links to item images.

What does it do?

Highlights items in your inventory that are currently equipped and adds instant quick equip/unequip clickable links to item images.

How to disable it

If you prefer to disable this functionality, you can easily turn it off:

  • Go to the Preferences page in-game.
  • Scroll down to the Hobo Helper Settings section.
  • Uncheck the relevant box and hit Save.
