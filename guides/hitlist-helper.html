Personal Hitlist Helper

Scans your hitlist to highlight online players green, calculates stat ranges, and injects a robust multi-column sorting capability with Experience tracking.

What does it do?

Scans your hitlist to highlight online players green, calculates stat ranges, and injects a robust multi-column sorting capability with Experience tracking.

How to disable it

If you prefer to disable this functionality, you can easily turn it off:

  • Go to the Preferences page in-game.
  • Scroll down to the Hobo Helper Settings section.
  • Uncheck the relevant box and hit Save.
