Market Helper

Enhance your shopping experience with large interactive buttons and integrated item thumbnails.

Upgraded Market Interface

The Market Helper automatically modernizes the SGHM and NPC Market pages by replacing the plain-text "Buy" links and navigation headers with large, styled buttons. It also natively injects high-quality 40x40 item thumbnails directly into the listings for Weapons, Armor, and Cart Parts.

The Market showing the injected item thumbnails and styled Buy buttons.

Tip: The Market Helper also automatically parses and structures the raw text output from the SGHM Market Watcher into a clean, sortable HTML table!
