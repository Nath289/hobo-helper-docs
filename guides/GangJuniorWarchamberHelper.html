Gang Junior Warchamber Helper

The Gang Junior Warchamber Helper modernizes and improves the user experience inside the Junior Warchamber by converting both the Offensive and Defensive unit dropdown lists into searchable interfaces.

Enhanced UI for Both Units

When you visit the Junior Warchamber, the standard member select dropdowns can be difficult to navigate. The script automatically converts the dropdowns for both the Offensive Unit and the Defensive Unit into searchable text boxes. Simply start typing a hobo's name or ID, and the list will filter dynamically.

Additionally, the Warchamber Tables themselves are restyled with alternating row colors, improved spacing, and clear hover effects, making it easier to read stats and arrange your fighters using the drag-and-drop handles.

Tip: The drag-and-drop reordering functionality operates exactly as before. Simply grab the handle (⋮⋮) on the far left of any row and pull it into position! The new auto-numbering system will recalculate your list dynamically.
