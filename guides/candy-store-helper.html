Candy Store Helper

Buy your lollipops and gummies faster.

What does it do?

Purchasing candy is an everyday task, but filling out the quantity boxes can be tedious. The Candy Store Helper injects "+ Buy Max" and "+ Buy 100" buttons right next to the purchase forms.

How to use it

  1. Visit the Candy Store.
  2. Click the + Buy Max button next to the candy you want.
  3. The script will automatically calculate how much candy you can afford with your on-hand cash, fill out the form, and submit it!
  4. Or, click + Buy 100 to instantly grab exactly 100 pieces.
