Recycling Bin Helper

Save time when tossing junk into the bin.

Quick-Add Buttons

Normally, getting rid of cans involves typing numbers into input boxes. The Recycling Bin Helper eliminates that by injecting quick-click "+100", "+500", etc. buttons right next to the usual inputs.

The Recycling Bin showing the Quick-Add buttons.

  1. Navigate to the Recycling Bin.
  2. Instead of manually entering the number of cans you wish to recycle, simply click one of the new Quick-Add buttons.
  3. The input box will instantly populate with your desired amount, ready for you to submit!
