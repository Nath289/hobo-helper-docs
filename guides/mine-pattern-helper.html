Mine Pattern Helper

Allows you to save a specific blast pattern coordinate sequence and displays it visually as ghost targets superimposed over the blast site.

What does it do?

Allows you to save a specific blast pattern coordinate sequence and displays it visually as ghost targets superimposed over the blast site.

How to disable it

If you prefer to disable this functionality, you can easily turn it off:

  • Go to the Preferences page in-game.
  • Scroll down to the Hobo Helper Settings section.
  • Uncheck the relevant box and hit Save.
